02 / DATA & SECRETS

Your data.
Your database.
Your keys.

Keep the database tools you like. Add the boundaries your application needs. Tsir is honest about what each driver can do—and refuses what it cannot.

  • ✓
    No pretend transactions

    Real transactions on Postgres. Atomic batches on D1.

  • ✓
    Migrations with a paper trail

    Drizzle’s journal, drift checks and explicit rollback.

  • ✓
    Encryption that fails closed

    Column-bound envelopes and resumable key rotation.

OPINIONATED, NOT OPAQUE

A few things we won’t compromise.

01

Explicit over ambient

Dependencies arrive through constructors. Transactions are handles you pass, not context you inherit.

02

Compiled over discovered

Routes become an immutable table. Production boot reads data instead of scanning your classes.

03

Refusal over pretending

Missing keys fail closed. Unsupported capabilities fail early. The framework says what it cannot do.

04

Evidence over adjectives

Package claims come with tests and documented failure modes. No invented benchmarks or customer counts.
